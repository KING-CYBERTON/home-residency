How it works

{{siteName}} is not a listings site. We choose, check, train and supervise every provider, and every booking runs through our team. You deal with us; we deal with the provider.

How we check providers

  • Everyone applies online for a specific service. Incomplete applications don't go forward.
  • We verify identity and documents with the issuing source, and speak to two referees.
  • The Certificate of Good Conduct is independently confirmed, not taken at face value.
  • A structured interview and a practical assessment, scored against a published standard.
  • Approval is made by a manager who did not do the vetting, and nobody works before finishing training.
  • Documents that expire are tracked; a provider is paused until they are renewed.

How a booking is handled

  • You choose the service, date, time and scope from the rate card. There is no haggling.
  • Our system ranks suitable providers by skill, distance, rating and availability; a person on our team makes the choice and records why.
  • For some services we may offer you a choice from a shortlist, showing skills, experience and rating.
  • The provider checks in on arrival with a code or their location, so you know they're there.
  • Any change to the work or price is approved in the app before it happens.
  • You confirm and rate the job. Late arrivals, low ratings and disputes are reviewed by our team every day.

If something goes wrong

  • Raise a complaint in your account. A low rating opens one automatically.
  • Safety and loss complaints are escalated at once and the provider is paused while we investigate.
  • We hear the provider's side before deciding, and we tell you what we did about it.
  • Outcomes range from a warning or retraining to removal from the platform.

Paying

You pay by M-Pesa against an invoice in your account. Providers are paid by us on a published cycle, so you never need to handle cash.

Privacy

We handle personal data under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. This summary explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Households: name, phone number, email, service addresses and access instructions, bookings, payments, ratings and complaints. Live-in placements also need an identity check.
  • Providers: identity and tax documents, Certificate of Good Conduct, referee feedback, qualifications, training and job records, and payout details.
  • Enquiries sent through this website: the details you type into the form.

How we protect it

  • Documents and sensitive fields such as gate codes are encrypted, and only staff whose role needs them can open them. Every access is logged.
  • Households and providers never see each other's contact details; messages go through us.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Contact us and we'll respond within the time the law sets.
